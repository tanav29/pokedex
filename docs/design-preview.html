WHAT IS DRIVING THE RETENTION

  • Variable rewards. Every pull is a fresh roll from a pool that grows with your level, and shinies sit at 1-in-48 — close enough to feel reachable, rare enough to chase.
  • A timer you can see. A Poké Ball every 20 minutes, counting down on the main screen. The app's job is to give you a reason to reopen it in an hour.
  • Streaks with real stakes. Daily rewards escalate for seven days and the streak lapses if you miss one. The strip says so out loud when yours is at risk.
  • Progress that is always visible. Level, XP, coins and ball counts sit above every screen, so no pull ever feels unaccounted for.
  • Nothing to claim. Quests pay out the instant they complete. A claim button is a chore that teaches people to forget their own rewards.
  • Unlocks, not walls. Levelling widens the species pool rather than gating a feature, so progress always opens somewhere new.
